Fixes.

• Tapping bare table no longer deals a card — only the stock itself does
• The rank in a card’s top corner no longer runs into the pips, or into the border on a Jack, Queen or King — most noticeable on the two-character “10”
• VoiceOver no longer offers the board behind the results screen: once a deal is won, the results are what a reader gets
• Resetting the statistics mid-deal no longer leaves the table reporting a game won and none played
• The menu always opens full height, so the deal number and the rules in force are there without dragging it up
• The menu rows that act on the game no longer wear a chevron promising a screen that never opens
• The Draw setting is labelled again — a segmented picker hides its own label, and the note underneath was naming a control that had none
• The Vegas balance in Statistics now counts the deal on the table, so it matches the figure in the status bar instead of showing a second, different one
• “Best score” now reads “Best score (Standard)”, so a Vegas player can see why the row is a dash
• At the largest text sizes the results panel keeps every figure on one line, the dead-end banner wraps its second line instead of cutting it off, and the auto-finish button stays a button rather than growing over the board
