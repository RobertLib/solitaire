No account or sign-in is required, and there is nothing to unlock — tap Play and
the whole game is available on the first launch.

NO GAMBLING OF ANY KIND. Nothing is wagered and nothing pays out. The app has no
StoreKit code and no networking code, so it cannot transact with anything, and
there is no way to buy, earn or transfer anything.

The one thing worth explaining is the optional "Vegas" scoring mode, which is a
scoring convention rather than a gambling mechanic: the deal starts the score at
-52 and each card played to a foundation adds 5, so a finished deal scores +208.
No bet is placed, no odds are offered, no balance is topped up and nothing can be
cashed out — it is a score that happens to be counted in dollars, exactly as the
original desktop version of the game counted it. The other two scoring modes
(Standard and none) do not use it at all. The Chance-Based Activities section of
the age rating questionnaire is therefore answered None, and the Gambling
declaration in App Information is No.

Fully offline. All content is generated at runtime — the cards are drawn with
shapes and the sounds are synthesised at launch, so the app ships with no audio
or image assets and makes no network requests. Settings, statistics and a game in
progress are stored in UserDefaults on the device (declared in
solitaire/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy, reason CA92.1).

How to play: drag a card onto a legal pile, or tap it to send it to the best
legal spot. Tap the stock to draw. The bottom bar has New Game, Undo, Hint and —
once the deal can no longer be lost — an auto-finish wand. The menu offers Draw 1
or Draw 3, the scoring mode, four table felts, four card backs and a left-handed
layout. Closing the app mid-deal saves it; reopening resumes the same deal with
its undo history intact.

Localisation: English and Czech, switched by the device language. There is no
in-app language setting.

The app supports iPhone and iPad in both portrait and landscape.
